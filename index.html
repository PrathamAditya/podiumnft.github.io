FAQ

Frequently asked scoops

What is sweetCone?

sweetCone is an NFT collection of 5,555 colourful cones. Every cone is put together from hundreds of different, hand-picked traits: scoops, cones, faces, accessories and backgrounds.

What do I get for owning one?

Access to the community of other sweetCone owners, where you can interact with each other, and early-bird access to upcoming projects.

Is the Cone Lab a mint?

Nope! The Cone Lab is a toy, just for fun. Cones you build there are pictures, not NFTs, and they don't reserve anything in the collection. The "Lab #" is just a fun number for your creation.

Where can I follow along?

Follow @sweetconenft on Twitter. Discord is coming soon.

How do I get in touch?

Drop a line to contact@sweetcone.art. We read everything, sprinkles included.
